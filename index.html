How it works

Three taps between you and a good conversation.

Wander is for the in-between moments: after dinner, on a lunch break, when the house feels too quiet.

  1. 1

    Open the app

    See who's walking, or who's up for a walk, near you right now. Nothing scheduled, nothing to plan.

  2. 2

    Tap to join

    Pick someone heading your way. Say hi, share a meeting spot, and head out the door as you are.

  3. 3

    Just walk and talk

    Talk about your day, the weather, anything at all. Walk as long as you like, and head home whenever you want.

Safety, built in

Relaxed doesn't mean careless.

Meeting someone new should feel easy, and it should feel safe. These features are part of the app from day one, not buried in settings.

Women-only mode

Filter by gender

Turn on gender-only mode and you'll only see, and only be seen by, people of the same gender. Women can keep every walk women-only.

One-tap SOS

The SOS button stays on screen for the whole walk. One press shares your live location with emergency contacts and gets you help fast.

Leave anytime

Unmatch or leave a walk with one tap, no explanation needed. You never owe anyone more of your time than you want to give.
